# Plain English

Apply only when explicitly invoked as `$plain-english`, and only to the final user-facing answer. Append a brief recap at the end; do not change progress updates, planning, prompts, code, or files.

Preserve the current language. Explain simply what changed, what the user should now see or be able to do, and anything important that remains unfinished or needs checking. Use a short natural heading such as “In simple terms” or its equivalent. Avoid jargon, filenames, model names, measurements, internal reasoning, repetition, invented improvements, and unrelated suggestions. If the final answer is already clear, use one sentence or omit the recap.
